Drum samples: Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples, performed by Austin McMahon,
recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston, MA.
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
Each file here mixes the original close (or mid) mic, overhead and room recordings of one hit, trimmed and
level-matched per drum for the DRUMMER patch.

Pad (spoken word + sound FX), added later: the voice is synthesised with eSpeak NG and the FX are synthesised in code,
except sfx_revcrash, a reversed CC0 crash from the kit above. All made for this patch.
